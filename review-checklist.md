# Documentation Review Checklist

## Pre-Authoring Analysis

- Scope is clear.
- Existing related docs have been identified.
- Needed SMEs or reviewers are known.
- Open questions are captured before writing begins.
- Risks and dependencies are documented.

## Language

- Grammar and spelling are correct.
- Tone matches house style.
- Terminology is consistent.
- Sentences are concise and unambiguous.

## Technical

- Steps match actual product behavior.
- Commands and code samples are valid.
- Preconditions and limitations are stated.
- Version-specific details are correct.
- Any differences between preview and production environments are called out.

## Structural

- Heading hierarchy is valid.
- Topic fits the expected template.
- Links point to the right destinations.
- Content is easy to scan.
- Duplicate content is avoided.
